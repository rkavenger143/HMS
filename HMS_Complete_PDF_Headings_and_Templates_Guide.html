1. Standard Universal Headings (Required on Every PDF) Global Standard

🏢 Institutional Header Elements

  • Hospital Name & Registered Logo
  • Accreditations: NABH / JCI / NABL & Hospital Reg. No.
  • Address & Contact: Street, City, State, PIN, Emergency Helpline, Website
  • Document Title: e.g., PRESCRIPTION, DISCHARGE SUMMARY, TAX INVOICE
  • Document Metadata: Document ID, Issue Date & Time, Barcode/QR Code

👤 Patient Demographic Bar (Top Ribbon)

  • UHID / Patient ID: Unique Hospital Identification No.
  • Patient Full Name, Age, Sex, Blood Group
  • Consulting Doctor & Clinical Department
  • IPD No. / Admission No. / Bed & Ward No. (for Inpatients)
  • Insurance / TPA Sponsor & Policy ID (for insured patients)
2. OPD (Outpatient Department) Headings & Templates Outpatient Care

📄 Prescription (Rx) Slip Headings

  • Vital Signs at Triage: BP, Pulse, SpO2, Temp, Height, Weight, BMI
  • Chief Complaints & Duration: (e.g. Fever x 3 days, Cough x 1 week)
  • Clinical History & Drug Allergies: (Severe drug/food reaction notes)
  • Provisional / Final Diagnosis: (with ICD-10 Coding)
  • Prescription Drugs Table (Rx): Drug Name, Dose, Route, Frequency, Duration
  • Diagnostic Investigations Ordered: Lab tests, X-Ray, USG, ECG
  • General Advice & Diet Guidelines
  • Follow-up Review Date & Emergency Warning Signs
  • Doctor's Signature, Reg. No. & Digital Seal

📋 OPD Consultation Case Sheet Headings

  • History of Present Illness (HPI)
  • Past Medical, Surgical & Family History
  • General Physical Examination (GPE): PICLE findings
  • Systemic Examination: CVS, RS, CNS, Per Abdomen (PA)
  • Differential Diagnoses & Clinical Impression
  • Treatment & Referral Plan

3. IPD (Inpatient Department) Headings & Templates Inpatient Care

🏥 Inpatient Discharge Summary Headings

  • Admission Date & Time vs. Discharge Date & Time
  • Length of Stay (LOS) & Ward/Bed History
  • Type of Discharge: Normal / DAMA / LAMA / Transfer / Expired
  • Admitting vs. Final Diagnosis: (Primary & Secondary ICD-10)
  • Chief Complaints & Reason for Admission
  • Summary of Clinical Course & Hospital Treatment Given
  • Significant Diagnostic, Lab & Imaging Findings
  • Surgeries & Procedures Performed: Date, Surgeon, Findings
  • Condition at Discharge: Vitals, Wound status, Mobility
  • Discharge Medications Schedule (Rx): Full Drug Table
  • Dietary & Physical Activity Restrictions
  • Wound Care & Catheter/Drain Management
  • Emergency "Red Flag" Symptoms Requiring Immediate Visit
  • Follow-up Clinic Schedule & Doctor Signatures

🛏️ IPD Admission Slip & OT Record Headings

  • IPD Admission Slip: IPD No., Admitting Unit, Room Category, Kin Details, Initial Advance Deposit
  • Daily Doctor Rounds Sheet: Subjective Assessment, Objective Vitals, Clinical Impression, Daily Plan
  • Operation Theatre (OT) Surgical Notes: Pre/Post-Op Diagnosis, Surgical Team, Anesthesia Type, Incision, Operative Steps, Implants Used, Sponge Count Verification

4. Nursing & Ward Management Headings & Templates Ward Operations

💉 Medication Administration Record (MAR)

  • Prescribed Medication & Strength
  • Route & Frequency (IV, Oral, SC, IM)
  • Scheduled Dosage Time Slots (06:00, 14:00, 22:00)
  • Administered Status: Given / Omitted / Withheld / Refused
  • Administering Nurse Initials & Exact Timestamp

📊 Vitals (TPR) & Fluid Balance (I/O)

  • Temperature, Pulse, Blood Pressure, SpO2 Matrix
  • Blood Glucose (CBG / RBS) & Pain Score (0-10)
  • Intake: Oral, IV Infusion, Blood Products (Total mL)
  • Output: Urine, Drains, Ryle's Tube, Vomitus (Total mL)
  • 24-Hour Cumulative Fluid Balance (+ / - mL)

🔄 Nursing Handover Sheet (SBAR)

  • S - Situation: Patient name, bed no, current active problems
  • B - Background: Clinical diagnosis, past medical history
  • A - Assessment: Current vitals, IV lines, wounds, labs
  • R - Recommendation: Pending tests, due medications

✅ Inpatient Discharge Checklist

  • Cannula & Catheter Removed Verification
  • Medicines & Discharge Summary Handed Over
  • Investigation Reports Handed to Attendant
  • Financial & Pharmacy Clearance Confirmed

  • Clinical Indication & Referring Physician

  • Systematic Radiological Findings
  • Impression / Definitive Conclusion
  • Radiologist Digital Signature & License No.

6. Billing, Accounts & Pharmacy Tax Invoices Financial & Accounts

💰 IPD Final Itemized Bill & Tax Invoice

  • Invoice Number, GSTIN & Tax Category
  • Bed & Nursing Charges: Rate/day × Number of Days
  • Consultation & Specialist Visit Fees
  • Operation Theatre (OT) & Anesthesia Charges
  • Diagnostic Lab & Radiology Investigation Charges
  • Pharmacy Drugs & Surgical Consumables
  • Total Gross Amount, Authorized Concession / Discount
  • Advance Payments & Interim Receipts Adjusted
  • TPA / Insurance Approved Amount & Co-Pay
  • Net Payable / Refund Amount (in Figures & Words)

💊 Pharmacy Dispensation Tax Invoice

  • Drug License Numbers: Form 20B / 21B
  • Itemized Drug Table: Drug Name, Batch No., Expiry Date, HSN, Quantity, MRP, Discount, GST %, Total
  • GST Breakdown Table: CGST, SGST, IGST Subtotals
  • Pharmacist Signature & Hospital Stamp
7. Blood Bank, Dietetics & Emergency / MLC Reports Specialty Modules

🩸 Blood Bank Cross-Match Report

  • Recipient Blood Group & Rh Type
  • Donor Bag Unit No. & Blood Component Type
  • Major & Minor Cross-Match Compatibility
  • TTI Screening Status: HIV, HBV, HCV, Syphilis, Malaria
  • Issue Timestamp & Transfusion Instructions

🥗 Clinical Diet Chart & Meal Card

  • Diet Category: Diabetic, Renal, Low-Sodium, Soft, NPO
  • Daily Caloric & Protein Targets (kcal / g)
  • Meal-by-Meal Schedule: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
  • Special Food Restrictions & Food Allergies

🚨 Emergency Medico-Legal (MLC) Sheet

  • MLC Registration Number & Police Station Informed
  • Arrival Mode & Accompanying Person / Officer
  • History of Incident: RTA, Assault, Poisoning, Burns
  • Detailed Wound & Injury Chart (Nature & Size)
  • Casualty Medical Officer (CMO) Signature & Seal

🚑 Ambulance Trip & Paramedic Run Sheet

  • Ambulance Unit No. & Paramedic Crew Names
  • Dispatch, Scene Arrival & Hospital Arrival Timestamps
  • Patient Condition at Pickup vs. Hospital Handover
  • En-Route Interventions: O2, Defibrillation, IV Fluids
